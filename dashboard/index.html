Implied value is the entry's own claim, not a forecast that a token launches. The odds are the peer record's base rate, not a promise. Farm economics assume a $10,000 position, a 7% airdrop share of implied value and a 4% T-bill as the alternative.

Live token peers

Protocols whose token already trades. They are excluded from the radar's ranking and supply the market-cap/TVL record the value curve is fitted on. Sorted by market cap.

#ProtocolCategoryTVLMarket cap7dMcap / TVL

A token record here means DefiLlama records a market cap. Protocols with a token record but no market cap are not peers and are counted separately.

Method & tests

Two fitted models. Every term is added to a flat baseline and kept only when it improves both blind tests.

The value model

implied = TVL × exp(a + b × ln TVL + category)

The term-by-term results are on the board. Kept terms are in the final model; the rest were tested and dropped.

The token odds

odds = logistic(size, age, growth, category rate)

Lower Brier is better; the board shows the term-by-term ablation and the base rate.

Held-out calibration

Every dot is a live-token protocol priced by the value curve that never saw it. The dashed diagonal is a perfect call; colour is the confidence band.

Range honesty

Farm economics

A $10,000 position over 12 weeks. Drop = implied value × 7% × position ÷ TVL. Fees are the plan's gas. The benchmark is the same money in a 4% T-bill for 12 weeks.

Limits

  • The snapshot is one day; TVL moves and the band is wider than one point estimate.
  • Market cap is circulating value, not fully diluted.
  • The odds are the peers' record, not a prediction that this protocol launches.
  • Past coverage is not a chance that a pre-token protocol reaches its band.
