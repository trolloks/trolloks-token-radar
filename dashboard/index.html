Implied value = launch value × launch odds. The odds are the peer record's base rate, not a promise. Farm economics assume a $10,000 position, a 7% drop share and a 4% T-bill as the alternative.

Live token peers

Protocols whose token already trades. They are excluded from the radar's ranking and supply the market-cap/TVL record the value curve is fitted on. Sorted by market cap.
