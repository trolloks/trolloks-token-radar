Live token peers

Protocols whose token already trades. They are excluded from the radar's ranking and supply the market-cap/TVL multiples the model uses. Sorted by market cap.

Method & backtest

One formula, three snapshot inputs, and a held-out check that reports how wrong the model has been.

The formula

implied = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity

  1. Peer multiple — the geometric median market-cap/TVL of live-token protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the all-market median with 5 pseudo-peers. A category with one live peer does not set the price alone.
  2. Growth — exp(0.3 × clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%)). A +20% week lifts value about 6%; a crash lowers it.
  3. Maturity — 0.6 + 0.4 × min(age, 3 years) / 3 years. A protocol with a three-year record without a token carries less execution risk than one listed last month.

Nothing is keyed to a name. Every term comes from the row's own TVL, listed date and 7-day change, and from peers in the same category.

What the board holds

Limits

  • The snapshot is one day. TVL moves; the band is wider than a single point estimate suggests.
  • Market cap is circulating value, not fully diluted. A launch can price differently.
  • The watchlist is a floor, not a universe: every pre-token record is ranked, including 302 below the $50M watchlist floor.
  • Past coverage is not a probability that a pre-token protocol reaches its band. Many never launch.
